# retention-toolkit

Merged 2026-09-29 from: thebranding.ai "30 Ways to STOP the Scroll" (IG carousel DdjRg6iDPNp, 8 slides read in full), Ava's "unskippable hook" reel (Context → Challenge → Transition), the Caleb Ralston × Hormozi personal-brand study (Downloads PDF), and `social-growth-science`. Load `social-growth-science` + `marketing-psychology` too — this skill adds the technique library and beat-mapping, it does not replace them.

## 0. Non-negotiables (from the user)
- **Real facts only.** A hook that needs a number, a mistake, a "day one" or a deadline must use one that really happened (Episode Log / `Experiments/`). Use `[INSERT]` until it's real. Techniques 06, 21, 22, 24 are the dangerous ones — the carousel's own examples ("Only 2.7%…", "8K vs 8M") are illustrations, never data to reuse.
- **One CTA per video**, tied to the money path (done-for-you AI video service DM, affiliate link, lead magnet). Judge by saves/sends/DMs, not views.
- English first for the personal brand; Alwazour store reels are Arabic (Levantine) and follow the Alwazour Reel OS.
- Any presenter shown is the AI presenter → keep the platform AI label + disclosure line.

## 1. The spine (every video)
`Hook (0–3s) → Retain → Reward → Open loop.`
Opener formula for story/series videos (Ava): **Context** (get the viewer up to date, one line) → **Challenge** (your goal + stakes) → **Transition sentence** into the meat. Pattern: concrete situation + hard goal + "day one" implies a follow-up. Use it when there is a real goal with real stakes; skip it for pure tutorials (use a result-first hook instead).

## 2. The 30 techniques, in 5 families (what each is FOR)
| Family | # and technique | Use when | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Verbal loops** (open curiosity) | 01 Open a loop · 02 Contradict the obvious · 03 Hide the payoff · 04 Introduce a mystery · 05 Ask for a guess | hook + first 10s; ~every 10–15s a smaller loop | every loop MUST close with a specific payoff; vague close = bait-and-switch (kills next post) |
| **B. Turns & proof numbers** | 06 Reveal a number · 07 Create an exception · 08 Add a twist · 09 Raise a question · 10 Open another loop | mid-video re-hook (the "hump") | number must be real and sourced |
| **C. Visual/edit interrupts** | 11 Break the pattern · 12 Punch in · 13 Remove everything (near-empty frame) · 14 Add movement · 15 Switch the angle | attention-drop moments; visual change every 2–4s | more than ~5 strong interrupts per short feels chaotic; <3 feels flat |
| **D. Proof & texture** | 16 Show proof (result/comment/screenshot) · 17 Change the pace · 18 Introduce a face · 19 Visual metaphor · 20 Interrupt yourself ("wait—look at this") | credibility beats; ties to "receipts" in the plan | proof must be real (Bencivenga: demonstrate, don't claim) |
| **E. Story structure & endings** | 21 Raise the stakes · 22 Reveal the mistake · 23 Show progress · 24 Make a comparison · 25 Mini payoff · 26 Countdown · 27 Challenge them · 28 Callback the hook · 29 Pause before the reveal · 30 Open a new question | build → reward → loop ending | close one loop, open the next (series glue); callback (28) makes a rewatch |

Family fit to the plan: our three formats (AI news tested / I tested it / Building) live on A+E; the "mistake / decision / win" weekly moments map to 22, 21, 25.

## 3. Beat map (fill this before scripting)
| Length | Hook | Interrupts | Loops | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short 25–45s | 0–3s: result or tension, first frame mid-action | 3–5 strong (family B/C/D), visual change every 2–4s | one small loop at ~10–15s | close hook loop + one CTA + loop back to line 1 (28) |
| Long 6–10 min | 0–30s: Context→Challenge→Transition | re-hook every 60–90s (10/25/30) | loop per chapter | mini payoff (25) + next-episode loop (30) |
Write the map as a table: `time | what viewer sees | what viewer hears | technique # | loop opened/closed`.

## 4. Retention QA (before publish and after 24h)
- Frame 1 already in motion; no logo intro, no "hey guys", no wide establishing shot.
- Sound-off test: payoff lands with captions + text only.
- Every opened loop has a mapped close. List them; count must match.
- Retention curve shape (read after posting): **cliff** = hook problem → recut first line/frame; **hump** = mid re-hook working; **plateau** flat & loop-friendly end = target. Fix the exact second people drop.
- **Benchmark conflict (unresolved):** `social-growth-science` says first-3s skip >40% = broken; a 2026 source says <20% healthy/<15% exceptional. Use the account's own baseline + the curve shape, not either absolute number, until we have our own data.
- Platform facts used: watch time incl. replays is the top Reels signal; DM sends carry the most weight for reaching non-followers; a 15s Reel watched 3× can beat a 60s watched once → design for the loop/rewatch. Trial Reels (schedulable since Feb 2026) can test 4–7 hooks/week before the main feed.
- Never cross-post an un-recut hook; re-shoot/re-cut first 3s per platform.

## 4b. Growth loop — Ava's "zero to 1M" method (reel Dd1JXyKyASb, 36.8 s, studied 2026-09-29; account @personalbrandlaunch, ~1M followers per search result)
Her three steps, verbatim in substance: **(1) Research:** Instagram Explore → search your niche → Reels tab → SAVE 100 videos with >1M views. **(2) Script:** take the hooks + general topics from those videos, write **30 scripts** = viral hook + YOUR niche expertise as the value; post **every day on ONE account**; repeat until the first viral video. **(3) Double down:** keep the hook and topic that hit, change the formats, keep posting.
- **What we take:** the loop (save 100 → 30 scripts → daily → double down) as the REACH engine; hook STRUCTURE from swipe videos, never their content; one account; change format, not topic, after a hit. Her CTA mechanic "comment the word X for the full thing" = keyword-comment → DM automation, which we already scoped (`DM_AUTOMATION_SETUP_2026-09-28.md`).
- **Caveats (say them):** the method is her own claim in a reel — nothing here independently verifies it produced her followers; "views" ≠ customers (Caleb Ralston) so we judge by saves/sends/DMs; the plan says YouTube main + IG repost, so run the loop on Shorts/Reels reposts of the weekly long video, not IG-only; every hook we borrow must still be filled with a REAL fact (§0).
- **How to run it here:** swipe library at `Second_Brain/Workflow/30 - Resources/` (hook | source account | views | technique # from §2 | why it worked) → 30-script batch = the 30-day plan → after each post log retention curve shape → after a hit, clone hook+topic into 3 new formats (talking head / screen record / carousel).
