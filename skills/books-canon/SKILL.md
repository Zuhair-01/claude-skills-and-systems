## Rules

- **Each entry states what is dated** — files `02`, `09`, `10`, `12` mark dead tactics explicitly. Do not ship those.
- **File `13` is the honesty layer** — it flags retracted (Wansink) and **dead** (ego-depletion) findings and rates every effect's confidence. Vet a claim there before it reaches a deck, an ad, or a price.
- **Where the canon disagrees with itself**, the files state the tension rather than picking a side — Sharp's empiricism vs Ries/Trout's differentiation; Binet & Field's long-brand view vs the growth-hacking school. Hold both.
- **Read for a decision, not for coverage.**
- **Never fork this content.** Update the numbered file in the vault in place and log the revision in `OpenCode Memory Log.md`.
